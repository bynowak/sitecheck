# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Treat others with respect, assume good intent, and keep feedback focused on ideas and behavior.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical discussion, patience with newcomers, accepting corrections, and respecting privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are unacceptable.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related interactions. Report concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub; ask for a private contact channel without public personal details if necessary. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn contributors, or restrict participation based on the severity and pattern of behavior. They will consider context and protect reporter privacy where possible.
